# 6. Report Findings
Chosen Scenario 
  - FC building elevator waiting times among students

Data Collection Process
  - The group utilized Google Forms to collect data, where respondents were asked to provide their observed elevator waiting times. A total of 30 responses were collected for this study.

Computed Parameters
- The mean elevator waiting time was calculated using the 30 responses collected through Google Forms which is 4.0667 minutes. For an exponential distribution, the mean is calculated as 1 divided by the rate parameter. Therefore, the rate parameter was calculated by taking the reciprocal of the mean:

- Lambda = 1 / 4.0667 = 0.2459 per minute

- Using this value, the Probability Density Function (PDF) is:

- f(x) = 0.2459e^(-0.2459x), where x is greater than or equal to 0.

- The Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF) is:

- F(x) = P(X is less than or equal to x) = 1 - e^(-0.2459x)

- These functions describe the distribution of the observed elevator waiting times and can be used to determine the probability of waiting for a specific amount of time or within a given time interval.


Real-World Implications
  - These findings can be useful in understanding the waiting experience of students using elevators. Longer waiting times may affect students who need to reach their classrooms or other destinations on time, particularly during busy periods. The results can therefore provide an indication of how frequently students may experience longer elevator waits.
